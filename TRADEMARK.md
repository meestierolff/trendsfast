# TrendsFast trademark policy

The GNU Affero General Public License covers copyright permissions in the
software. It does **not** grant rights to the TrendsFast name, logo, domain,
trade dress, or other source-identifying marks (the “Marks”).

This policy is an alpha draft and must be reviewed by the founder and qualified
legal counsel before a public commercial launch. It is not legal advice.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may make truthful, nominative statements such as:

- “uses TrendsFast” or “compatible with TrendsFast”;
- “a fork of the TrendsFast open-source project”;
- links to the official repository or documentation;
- screenshots that accurately identify the unmodified project.

Do not imply sponsorship, certification, or endorsement.

## Uses that require written permission

- naming a product, company, service, package, domain, or account “TrendsFast”
  or confusingly similarly;
- using the logo or trade dress as your own product identity;
- presenting a modified deployment as the official hosted service;
- merchandising or paid promotion centered on the Marks.

Forks must use a distinct name and visual identity, clearly disclose that they
are unofficial, and preserve copyright/license notices. Descriptive references
to the upstream project remain welcome.

## Fair use and contact

Nothing here is intended to restrict uses permitted by applicable trademark
law. Until a dedicated legal contact is published, open a repository discussion
for non-confidential permission questions. Do not disclose sensitive legal or
security information in public issues.
