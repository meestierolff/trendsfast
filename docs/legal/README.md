# Alpha legal documents

The files in this directory are drafting checklists, **not approved public legal
policies and not legal advice**. They contain placeholders and cannot be shipped
unchanged.

Before accepting real submissions or payments, the founder and qualified legal
counsel must determine the operating legal entity, contact/address,
jurisdictions, controller/processor roles, lawful bases, age/geography limits,
consumer/subscription rules, provider data rights, subprocessors, international
transfers, retention, deletion, and dispute terms.

- [Privacy notice alpha template](PRIVACY_ALPHA_TEMPLATE.md)
- [Terms alpha template](TERMS_ALPHA_TEMPLATE.md)

Repository maintainers must not mark legal permission “verified” based on code,
provider account creation, or these templates. Reddit automation remains
`LEGAL_REVIEW` regardless of template completion.
