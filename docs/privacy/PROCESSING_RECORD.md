# Processing record (RoPA) working draft

This is an internal engineering inventory, not legal approval, a published
privacy notice, or legal advice. The founder and qualified counsel must confirm
the controller/processor roles, entity, contacts, lawful bases, jurisdictions,
transfer safeguards, statutory retention, and final subprocessor list before
real-user launch. No placeholder below is an approved fact.

Mailboxes such as `privacy@`, `security@`, `support@`, and `billing@` are
deployment requirements to provision, monitor, and test; this record does not
claim they exist. Record the approved owner, backup owner, effective date, and
release SHA privately before launch.
