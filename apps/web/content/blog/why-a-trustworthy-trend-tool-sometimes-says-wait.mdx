The easiest way to make a trend product look productive is to return an idea every time. It is also the fastest way to make the product generic.

TrendsFast treats `WAIT` as a first-class result. It means the system completed its work and found that no action cleared the quality floor.

## Thin evidence should stop the move

A useful distribution decision needs more than a topic. It needs enough relevance, freshness, credibility, evidence quality, channel fit, and founder fit to justify acting now.

Any one of those can fail:

- the apparent momentum comes from one snapshot;
- several receipts copy the same origin;
- the conversation is fresh but unrelated to the product’s buyer;
- the founder has no credible point of view;
- the available format cannot be produced while the window is open;
- the channel is saturated with interchangeable takes.

In each case, forcing a hook would hide the actual result.

## WAIT is more specific than “no”

A good WAIT result explains what prevented action and what would change the decision. It can preserve the strongest query cluster, name the missing independent source, recommend a re-check window, or identify the product context that needs founder correction.

That makes WAIT operational. An agent can schedule a bounded follow-up without publishing weak content in the meantime.

## The quality floor protects the founder

Founders borrow credibility every time they make a strong timing claim. “This is taking off” is expensive language when the receipts show only one popular post. A system that sometimes stays quiet protects that credibility.

The same floor also controls provider and model cost. If required coverage is missing, the scan should surface degraded coverage or stop before its cost ceiling—not buy more calls until it finds a convenient answer.

## Success is a defensible decision

The product outcome is not “one content idea per day.” It is one decision the founder can understand and inspect. Sometimes that decision is PUBLISH, REPLY, or REMIX. Sometimes it is WAIT.

If the evidence does not support action, saying so is not a failure to generate. It is the feature working.
