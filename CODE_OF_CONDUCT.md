# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the TrendsFast community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive
feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention,
trolling or insults, public or private harassment, publishing private
information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
code, issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code and
will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct privately to the project maintainers through a non-public
contact channel listed on the repository profile. Do not use the vulnerability
channel unless the report also presents a security risk. All reports will be
reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy
and safety as far as reasonably possible.

Consequences may range from a private correction or warning through temporary
or permanent exclusion, based on impact, intent, recurrence, and repair.
